## Notes (paste this)

Thank you for reviewing **麻將16張 (Mahjong 16)**.

**What it is**
- A **single-player** Taiwanese 16-tile mahjong game. You play one seat against **three AI opponents**. No account, no login, no network connection is required — the game is fully playable offline and startable in one tap.

**How to play (no setup needed)**
1. Launch the app — the home screen shows a large **"Start Playing" (開始遊戲)** button.
2. Tap it — a full four-player table is dealt **immediately** (you + 3 AI). There is no lobby, no matchmaking, and no waiting for other players.
3. On your turn, tap a tile in your hand, then tap **Discard (出牌)**. When you can claim a tile, use the **Chi / Pong / Kong / Hu (吃/碰/槓/胡)** buttons; tap **Pass (過)** to skip.
4. When a hand ends, a **settlement screen** shows the winning hand and the scoring (tai). Tap **Next (下一局)** to play another hand, or **Exit (退出)** to return to the home screen.
5. The home screen also has a **"How to Play / Rules" (麻將教學 / 規則)** screen that explains the rules and scoring in both Chinese and English.

**Key points for review**
- **Single-player only.** The three opponents are built-in AI. There is no online multiplayer and no real-money feature of any kind.
- **No in-app purchases, no ads, no accounts, no user-generated content, no data collection.**
- A full round can be completed by one person without any external dependency.
- The game is a traditional skill-based tile game; there is no wagering.

**Languages:** Traditional Chinese (primary) and English (in-app toggle at the top-right of the home screen).

**Contact:** io@xoio.io
